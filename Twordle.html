How to Play Twordle v3.1

Guess the hidden five-letter word in six attempts.

  • Enter any valid five-letter word.
  • Press Enter to submit your guess.
  • Each guess provides colour-coded clues.

Colour Clues

Green - Correct letter in the correct position.

Yellow - Correct letter in the wrong position.

Grey - Letter is not in the word.

Additional Features

  • The keyboard at the bottom helps you keep track of all 3 colours.
  • Current Word lets you keep track of correctly guessed letters in their right spots.
  • If you guess 5 invalid words in the same round, you lose that game.
  • Clear Stats to erase your history and start again.

Cheat Features

  • Hint will reveal the first unguessed letter, but not the whole word. This is the least offensive cheat.
  • Evaluate will test a word to see how much it reduces the results, but be careful. It let's you test invalid words.
  • Cheat Shows you all possible matches. You have to be really stumped to use this.
  • Undo removes the last played word. This is the most egregious cheat.

Privacy

This page uses localStorage/cookies to save those stats and for no other reason.
